## Repository workflow and H4rmony SFT planning

The local checkout was fast-forwarded to repository commit
`613693f414ddd105149d694ecc66b17bf7b5a02f`, which includes the August 25
H4rmoniousCaramel matched-checkpoint pilot. A repository-level `AGENTS.md` was
added to make two working conventions persistent for future sessions: completed
task changes should be committed unless the user explicitly opts out, and
substantive work should be recorded in the dated research log in the same commit.

The H4rmony source dataset supplies three ranked completions for each prompt. R1
is the preferred, most environmentally aligned response; R2 is the ambivalent
response; and R3 is the least environmentally aligned response. The pairwise
source rows compare R1 with R2 and R1 with R3. The proposed SFT intervention uses
only one R1 completion per prompt; “R1” does not refer to the DeepSeek-R1 model.

The H4rmony project's public `data/train.csv` contains 517 SFT rows covering 512
unique prompts. Five prompts occur twice. The target outputs are short, averaging
about 123 characters, so the R1-only corpus is a very small SFT run.

For one R1 completion per unique H4rmony prompt, a dense 8B base model, LoRA rank
16, three epochs, BF16, gradient checkpointing, dynamic padding or sequence
packing, and a single A100, the revised provisional estimate is:

- roughly 3–10 minutes of optimization for two or three epochs; and
- approximately 15–35 minutes end to end once model download, tokenization,
  evaluation, and adapter saving are included, with up to 45 minutes budgeted for
  a cold or slower Colab session.

These are planning ranges rather than measured benchmarks. Before the full run,
the R1-only dataset should be deduplicated by prompt, tokenized with the exact
base tokenizer, and its mean, p95, and maximum sequence lengths recorded. A short
timed training sample should then replace the provisional estimate with measured
tokens per second. Because the completions are short, model loading and evaluation
may take longer than optimization itself.

LoRA rank 16 is a reasonable default for this narrow intervention. The small
dataset makes overfitting and generic response-style drift more immediate concerns
than adapter capacity. A useful initial configuration is rank 16, alpha 32,
dropout 0.05, two or three epochs, and checkpoints at least once per epoch, with a
matched base-model evaluation and non-ecological controls retained throughout.

## Clarification of R1 terminology

The initial planning discussion incorrectly interpreted “R1 answer” as a response
generated by DeepSeek-R1. The intended meaning is the H4rmony dataset's rank-1
completion: the most environmentally aligned of the three authored responses for
each prompt. The estimates and assumptions above have been corrected accordingly.
