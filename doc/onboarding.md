1. What is the project's central research question, and what competing explanations is it designed to distinguish?
2. What interventions, models, datasets, evaluations, results, and limitations define the project so far?
3. Latest session — 2026-09-11: What positive result and limitations are summarized in the September 10 planning log, what does a one-unit score increase mean, and how will the next experiments test direction-specific extremeness across values and models?
